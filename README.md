# Peters Crafts

Welcome to **Peters Crafts** – artisanal work & restoration by **Smidje Peter**.  
Here you can explore my projects, creations, and experiments in blacksmithing, woodworking, metalwork, and creative crafts.

## 🌟 About Me
I am **Peter Muls**, a craftsman passionate about:
- Blacksmithing and historical techniques
- Woodworking and metalworking
- Jewelry and knife making
- Restoration and repairs
- Technology and modern applications (Arduino, electronics, IoT)

With over 25 years of experience, I combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology.
